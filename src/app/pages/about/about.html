Our story

A workshop, not a factory

Ira Soaps started at a kitchen counter with one pot of oat-and-honey soap batter and a wire cutter. What we make has grown since then, but not how we make it — every bar, wash and concrete piece we sell is still mixed and finished by hand, in batches small enough that we know each one.

EST. IN A HOME KITCHEN

Now a small workshop, still small-batch.

Why chemical-free

We started with our own skin

The first bars were made to solve a personal problem — reactive, easily irritated skin that didn't get along with sulphates or synthetic fragrance. Cold-process soap-making and natural surfactant blends were the fix, and they still are: no parabens, no sulphates, no artificial fragrance, in anything we sell.

Why concrete art

The same hands-on instinct

Concrete art grew out of the same instinct as the soap — working with a raw material directly, seeing it change state, and finishing every piece by hand. Our vases, jars and candle vessels are poured the same way we pour soap: measured, mixed, poured, and cured before they ever reach a shelf.
